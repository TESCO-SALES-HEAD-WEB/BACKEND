Account & Data Deletion

Tesco Structures Sales CRM (Sales Coordinator, Sales Manager, Sales Head) · Developer: Tesco Structures

The Tesco Structures Sales CRM apps are internal business tools. User accounts are issued and managed by Tesco Structures administrators. If you are an authorised user and want your account and associated personal data deleted, you can request it as described below.

How to request account and data deletion

  1. Send an email to salestescostructures@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my account — Sales CRM.
  3. Include your full name and, if known, your employee ID so we can locate your account.
  4. Our team will verify the request and delete your account. We aim to action verified requests within 30 days.

You can also call us on +91 90033 28229 or +91 96006 52424 to make the request.

What data is deleted

On a verified request we delete your user account and associated personal data, including your name, email address, phone number, employee ID, and any device push-notification tokens linked to your account.

What data may be retained

Business records created in the course of our work — such as leads, appointments, quotations, orders, and payment/invoice records that relate to our customers and projects — may be retained where we are required to keep them for legitimate business, accounting, tax, or legal purposes, in line with applicable record-keeping requirements. Such records are retained only as long as necessary for those purposes.
