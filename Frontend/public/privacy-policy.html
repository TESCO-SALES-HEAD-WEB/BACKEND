Privacy Policy

Tesco Structures Sales CRM · Last updated: 12 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tesco Structures ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Tesco Structures Sales CRM — comprising the Sales Coordinator, Sales Manager, and Sales Head web and mobile applications (together, the "Apps"). The Apps are internal business tools used by our authorised staff to manage leads, appointments, quotations, orders, and payment collection.

Who can use the Apps

The Apps are intended for authorised employees and team members of our organisation who are issued login credentials. They are not intended for use by the general public or by children under 18.

Information we collect

Account information (about our users): name, email address, phone number, employee ID, role, and password (stored in encrypted/hashed form).

Device information for notifications: a push notification token generated by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) so we can deliver notifications to your device (mobile apps).

Business records entered in the Apps (about customers/leads): customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, project and site addresses, budgets, appointment details, quotation and order details, and payment/invoice information. This information is entered by our staff to carry out our business and is processed on behalf of our organisation.

Usage/log data: basic technical logs generated when the Apps communicate with our servers (for example, timestamps and error information).

How we use information

  • To authenticate users and provide access to the Apps.
  • To operate core features: managing leads, appointments, quotations, orders, and payments.
  • To send operational push notifications (for example, new lead assignments, appointment updates, quotation approvals, and payment reminders).
  • To maintain security, prevent misuse, and troubleshoot problems.

How information is stored and shared

Data is stored in our secured backend database (MongoDB Atlas) and transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. We use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver push notifications; a device push token and the notification content are processed by Google for that purpose (see Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy).

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information with third parties for advertising. Information is accessible only to authorised users within our organisation and to service providers that help us operate the Apps (such as our hosting and notification providers), who are bound to protect it.

Data security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and access restricted to authenticated users. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Apps and for legitimate business or legal purposes. Business records may be retained in accordance with our record-keeping requirements.

Your rights and data deletion

Because the Apps are issued to authorised staff, account creation and deletion are managed by our administrators. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account or associated data, contact us at the address below and we will act on your request in line with applicable law.

Children's privacy

The Apps are not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
